# Credits

## Texture Sources

All planet and moon texture maps are derived from the following sources:

### NASA / JPL

Original planetary imagery from NASA missions (Voyager, Cassini, MESSENGER, New Horizons,
Hubble Space Telescope, and others). These are in the public domain as works of the
United States government.

### Celestia Motherlode / Celestia Content Project

Processed texture maps for use in the Celestia space simulator.
Many maps are composites of NASA data processed by community contributors.

- License: Varies by contributor; most are free for non-commercial use.
- **⚠ Flag for review before public release**: Some individual maps may have
  specific attribution requirements.

### Three.js Examples

Texture maps included in the Three.js examples repository.
- License: MIT (same as Three.js)

### threex.planets by J. Hastings-Trew

Procedural and processed planet textures.
- Author: James Hastings-Trew
- License: Creative Commons — check specific version before commercial use.
- **⚠ Flag for review before public release**
